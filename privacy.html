Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to the CirkleUp app and this website.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information CirkleUp collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. CirkleUp is operated by cirkleUp LLC (“CirkleUp,” “we,” “us”).

Who CirkleUp is for

CirkleUp is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and accounts found to belong to someone under 18 are removed.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name or display name, username, email address, password (stored in protected, hashed form), date-of-birth confirmation, and profile details you choose to add, such as a photo and bio.
  • Content you create — posts, photos, videos, livestreams, comments, messages, event listings, and the metadata attached to them (such as the time they were posted).
  • Referral information — if you join through a referral link or code, we record who invited you so lineage and any referral bonus are credited correctly.
  • Purchase and earnings records — when you buy coins, tip, buy a ticket, or earn as a creator, we keep records of the transaction, the amount, and the shares owed. Payments themselves are processed by the app stores and our payment processors; we do not store your full card number.
  • Device and usage information — device type, app version, general usage activity, and crash or performance data, used to keep the app working and safe.
  • Verification information — if you request the Platinum Check, we review the public follower or subscriber count on the platform account you connect or show us.

How we use information

  • To run your account and show your content to the people who follow you, in the order you post it.
  • To record sales, calculate creator shares and referral bonuses, and pay out earnings.
  • To operate safety tools — reports, blocks, and moderation — and to enforce our Terms.
  • To communicate with you about your account, your payouts, and important changes to the service.
  • To understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve features.

What we share

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these cases:

  • Public by design — your profile, posts, and public activity are visible to other members, as with any social platform. Private messages are visible to the people in the conversation.
  • Service providers — companies that help us run CirkleUp (hosting, payments, payouts, analytics) receive only what they need to do their job.
  • Legal and safety reasons — if the law requires it, or to protect members, the public, or CirkleUp’s rights.
  • A business change — if cirkleUp LLC is ever sold or merged, information may transfer with the business, under the same protections in this policy.

Your choices and controls

  • Edit your profile and privacy-facing details in the app at any time.
  • Block any account, and report content, profiles, and lives from inside the app.
  • Request a copy of your account information, or deletion of your account and associated personal information, through the app’s settings or support channels. Some records — such as completed payment and payout records — may be kept where the law or tax rules require it.
  • Control notifications in the app and in your phone’s settings.

This website

This website is informational. It does not run advertising trackers. If we add standard analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No system is perfect, and we cannot promise absolute security — please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will post the updated version here and note it in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reach us through the support options inside the CirkleUp app, or write to cirkleUp LLC, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA.
