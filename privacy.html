CirkleUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

CIRKLE Up LLC (“CirkleUp,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the CirkleUp mobile applications and related services (together, the “Service”). CirkleUp is a social platform for creators, and sign-up is open to everyone — an invite code is optional and serves only as a referral path. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, password (stored in hashed/encrypted form by our authentication provider), and profile details you choose to add (such as a photo, bio, and links). If you sign up using an optional invite code, we also record which member referred you.
  • User content. We collect the content you create or upload through the Service: posts, photos, videos, audio, comments, messages, and shop product listings. Live audio and video on the Service are delivered through third-party streaming infrastructure (see §4).
  • Transaction information. If you buy a product from a member’s shop, or if you earn as a creator, we collect information about the transaction, such as what was purchased, the amount, and its date and status. CirkleUp does not offer subscriptions, tips, or digital products. Payments are processed by Stripe; we do not collect or store your full payment card number.
  • Usage and device information. We automatically collect information about how you use the Service, such as the features you use, content you view, the time and duration of your activity, device type and operating system, app version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and crash or diagnostic data.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, understand how the site is used, and protect against abuse. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work without them.
  • Information from others. Other members may provide information about you — for example, when a member refers you to CirkleUp using the optional invite code, or mentions you in content they post.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Service, including accounts, feeds, content hosting, shops, and live audio/video
  • Operate the optional referral program
  • Process transactions and calculate creator earnings and payouts
  • Communicate with you, including service announcements and responses to support requests
  • Keep the Service safe: detecting and preventing fraud, spam, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service, and reviewing reported content and accounts
  • Understand how the Service is used and improve features and performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Your content and visibility

Content you post is visible according to the audience settings of the Service and the choices you make. Please don’t post information you don’t want the relevant audience to see. Profile information such as your name, photo, and bio is visible to other members.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers. We use trusted third parties to run the Service. These providers process information only to provide their services to us. They are: Firebase (Google), which provides our backend, authentication, and database; Stripe, which processes payments for shop purchases and creator earnings and payouts; and ZEGOCLOUD, which provides live audio and video streaming.
  • Other members. Your profile and the content you share are visible to other members as described in §3. If you joined through a referral, the member who referred you, and members you refer, may be visible as part of your referral connections.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and property of CirkleUp, our users, or the public, or to enforce our Terms.
  • Business transfers. If CirkleUp is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you delete content, it is removed from active display, though copies may remain in backups for a limited period. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it by law (for example, transaction records).

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update much of your profile information in the app’s settings. To exercise other rights — including account deletion — contact us at darciaj2019@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

If you are in California, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

7. Children’s privacy

CirkleUp is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction, if higher), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at darciaj2019@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you use the Service from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the Service or by email before the changes take effect. The “Effective date” at the top shows when this version was published.
