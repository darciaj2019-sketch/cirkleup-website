5. Creator earnings and payouts

  • When fans spend coins with you — tips, gifts, and ticketed events — your share is recorded in dollars at the moment of the sale.

  • Hold: earnings become payable 7 days after they are earned.
  • Payouts: payouts are made weekly, once a creator’s payable balance reaches the $10 minimum.
  • Referral bonus: if a creator joined with your referral code, you receive 10% of that creator’s ticket sales during their first 30 days, taken from the creator’s share. The bonus goes to the direct inviter only. After the first 30 days, the creator keeps their full share.
