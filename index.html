The problem

You do the creating. They keep the money.

On the big platforms, the deal is tilted — and every creator knows it:

The algorithm decides

You post, and a ranking system decides whether your own followers ever see it. Your audience, their rules.

The platform takes the big cut

Coins, tips, and ticket money pass through the platform first — and the platform keeps a large share of every sale you make.

Your audience is stuck there

You spent years building your people on their app. Leave, and you start over from zero.

The fix

Built for the creator, first.

CirkleUp flips the deal: a fair split in writing, a feed that respects your people, and tools to run your own shows — all in one app.

The money, plain and simple

You keep 90%.

When a fan buys coins and spends them with you — tips, gifts, ticketed events — your share is recorded in dollars at the moment of the sale. CirkleUp keeps 10%. You keep the rest.

  • 90% is your share of what CirkleUp actually receives from your sales, after the app stores take their cut.
  • Cash out from $10 — a minimum a real creator can actually reach.
  • Paid weekly — earnings become payable after a 7-day hold, then go out in the weekly payout.
  • Referral bonus: when someone joins with your code and sells tickets, you receive 10% of their ticket sales in their first 30 days, from the creator’s share — direct inviter only.
