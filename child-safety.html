CirkleUp Child Safety Standards

Effective date: November 23, 2026

CIRKLE Up LLC (“CirkleUp,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the CirkleUp mobile applications and related services (together, the “Service”). This page publishes our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). These standards apply to all content and conduct on the Service and are enforced under our Terms of Service and community rules.

CirkleUp is for adults only

CirkleUp is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The Service is not directed at children, and children are not permitted to use the Service. If we learn that an account belongs to a child, we will remove the account.

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

CirkleUp has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content or conduct that endangers or exploits children. The following are strictly prohibited on the Service:

  • Creating, uploading, posting, sharing, or storing any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form, including photos, videos, audio, text, and computer-generated or illustrated material.
  • Soliciting, requesting, or offering CSAM, or linking to it anywhere else.
  • Grooming or any attempt to befriend, manipulate, or arrange contact with a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sexualizing a child in any content, comment, message, or live stream.
  • Any other content or conduct that endangers, exploits, or harms a child.

Pornographic content is also prohibited on the Service.

Child endangerment and exploitation sit at the top of our community rules. There is no warning and no second chance for these violations.

How to report a child safety concern

Anyone can report content or an account that may endanger or exploit a child:

  • In the app: use the report option on the content, profile, message, or live stream. You can report both content and accounts.
  • By email: contact our designated child safety contact at darciaj2019@gmail.com. Please include a link to or description of the content or account and any details that will help us find it.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement first, then report it to us.

How we respond

When we receive a report of content or conduct that may endanger or exploit a child, we act on it:

  • The reported content is reviewed and, where it violates these standards, removed.
  • The account responsible is permanently banned from the Service.
  • Where content appears to be child sexual abuse material or otherwise illegal, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or to law enforcement, as required by law.

We preserve information connected to a report as needed for these reports and for legal obligations.
