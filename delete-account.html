Delete Your CirkleUp Account

Effective date: November 16, 2026

CIRKLE Up LLC (“CirkleUp,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the CirkleUp mobile applications and related services (together, the “Service”). This page explains how to delete your CirkleUp account, what information is deleted when you do, and what information we may retain. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

How to delete your account in the app

You can delete your account from inside the CirkleUp app:

  1. Open the CirkleUp app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

Deleting your account is permanent. Once your account is deleted, you will no longer be able to sign in to that account or access its content through the Service.

How to request deletion by email

You can also request account deletion by contacting us at darciaj2019@gmail.com. Please contact us from, or tell us, the email address connected to your CirkleUp account so we can identify the account. As described in our Privacy Policy, to exercise your rights — including account deletion — contact us at darciaj2019@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

What is deleted

If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, as described in our Privacy Policy. This includes, for example:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile details you added, such as your photo, bio, and links.
  • User content — the content you created or uploaded through the Service, such as posts, photos, videos, audio, comments, messages, and shop product listings. When you delete content, it is removed from active display.

What may be retained, and for how long

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

After you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it by law (for example, transaction records).

When you delete content, it is removed from active display, though copies may remain in backups for a limited period.
