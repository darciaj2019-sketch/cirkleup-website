The FAQ

What CirkleUp is, how the money works, how the badges work, and how we keep the place safe — in plain language.

Getting started

What is CirkleUp?
CirkleUp is a social platform built for creators, from cirkleUp LLC in Las Vegas, Nevada. Post to a chronological feed, go live, play games with your community, host ticketed events, keep your work in a content library, and chat with your people — all in one app.
Is CirkleUp open now — and when is the big launch?
CirkleUp is open now — download the app and join today. The big launch celebration is New Year’s Eve, with Sterling hosting the midnight countdown live on CirkleUp. The first ones in are crowned as early believers — see the badges section below.
Who can join?
Anyone 18 or older. Sign-up is open — download the app, create a free account, and you are in. No invitation is required.
What does it cost to join?
Creating an account is free. Inside the app, fans can buy coins to support creators with tips, gifts, and event tickets — supporting the creators you love is always your choice.
What is a referral code? Do I need one?
A referral code is how CirkleUp knows who brought you. You do not need one to join. But if a creator or a friend sent you their link or code, use it when you sign up: you land already following them, and you are connected to them in their lineage.
What is lineage?
Your lineage is your place in the network you build. The people who join through your link or code stay connected to you in your lineage — a permanent community position that shows the network you brought together. It is about connection and credit for building your circle — nothing more, nothing hidden.

Coins, tips & payouts

How do creators make money on CirkleUp?
Fans buy coins in the app and spend them with you — as tips and gifts, and on tickets to your events. Every sale is recorded in dollars at the moment it happens, with your share written down right then.
What is the split — how much do I keep?
You keep 90% of what CirkleUp actually receives from your sales, after the app stores take their cut. CirkleUp keeps 10%. The same split covers coins, tips, gifts, and ticketed events.
When do I get paid?
Earnings become payable 7 days after they are earned — that hold lets refunds and reversed payments settle before money moves. Payouts then go out weekly.
Is there a minimum cash-out?
Yes — you can cash out once your payable balance reaches $10.
What is the referral bonus?
If a creator joins CirkleUp with your referral code and sells tickets, you receive a bonus of 10% of their ticket sales during their first 30 days. The bonus comes out of the creator’s share, and it goes to the direct inviter only — the one person whose code was used. After those first 30 days, the creator keeps their full 90% share.
Do you promise how much I will earn?
No — and be careful with any platform that does. What we put in writing is the split: your share of what your sales actually bring in. What you earn depends on your work and your audience.

Badges

What is the Crown?
The Crown is for the first ones in — the early believers. Join CirkleUp as one of the first people on the app and a gold crown sits by your name everywhere it shows — profile, posts, comments, chat, live, and events. There is no deadline and no window attached to it. No claiming, no applying — and you keep it forever. It is our loyalty to the people who believed in us.
What is the Platinum Check?
The Platinum Check is CirkleUp’s mark for the biggest creators: members with 1,000,000 or more followers or subscribers on any one major platform — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Kick, or Twitch. Request it in the app and connect or show the platform account; we verify the count once. It is never self-claimed — a check anybody could just take would not be worth wearing.
Once I earn a badge, can I lose it?
No. Once a badge is earned, it is permanent — the Platinum Check stays even if your follower count later dips, and the Crown never comes off. We do not take honors back.
Can I wear both badges?
Yes — the badges stack. A creator with a million followers who is also one of the first ones in wears the Crown and the Platinum Check side by side.

Safety & your account

How do I report something or block someone?
Every post, profile, and live has a report option, and you can block any account so it cannot reach you. Reports go to our moderation team for review. If someone is bothering you, block first — you never have to engage.
Is CirkleUp really 18+?
Yes. CirkleUp is for adults 18 and older, and accounts are expected to belong to the person using them — one person, one account.
Who runs CirkleUp?
CirkleUp is run by cirkleUp LLC, a company born in Las Vegas, Nevada. Sterling Cole and Claire Donovan are the faces of the front team — the hosts you will see at the parties and in the welcome materials.
Where can I read the rules and the privacy policy?
Right here: the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy are linked at the bottom of every page on this site, and inside the app.
