The full tour

Everything in the lights

Ten features, one deal: the creator comes first. This is the whole app, sign by sign.

Coins, Tips & Payouts

You Keep 90%

Fans support you with coins and tips, and your share is recorded in dollars at the moment of the sale. You keep 90% of what CirkleUp actually receives after the app stores’ cut — cash out from $10, paid weekly, after a 7-day hold.

The Feed

No Algorithm

Your posts appear in the order you post them — a chronological feed. No ranking system buries you, and no boost button decides who sees your work. Your people see you when you post.

Live, any time

Go Live

Go live for your people whenever you are ready — shows, hangouts, parties, and live events, all inside the app, with your community right there with you.

Play together

Gaming

Play and stream your games on CirkleUp with your community watching, chatting, and cheering along in real time.

Your show, your tickets

Ticketed Events

Host your own events and sell your own tickets. You set the show and the price; your audience buys in; your share follows the same 90% split.

Your work, kept

Content Library

Your videos and posts live in your content library, where your people can find them and save them any time. Your body of work stays reachable — it does not vanish down a feed.

Talk with your people

Chat & Community

Chat, comments, and community in one place. The conversation with your audience belongs to you — build your circle and keep it close.

Your link, your landing

Bring Your People

Share your referral link or code anywhere. When your people tap it and sign up, they land already following you — your audience arrives with you, not after you.

Your place in the network

Lineage

The people who come in through you stay connected to you in your lineage — your permanent place in the network you built, shown by your name wherever you appear.

👑
The Crown

Wear the Crown Forever

The first people on CirkleUp — the early believers — wear a gold Crown by their name, everywhere it shows. No deadline and no window: you believed in us early, so the Crown is yours and you keep it forever. Pair it with the Platinum Check if your audience earns that too — the badges stack.

How the money moves

Simple, in writing

  • A fan buys coins in the app and spends them with you — tips, gifts, and ticketed events.
  • Your share is recorded in dollars at the moment of the sale. No mystery math later.
  • You keep 90% of what CirkleUp actually receives, after the app stores take their cut. CirkleUp keeps 10%.
  • Earnings become payable after a 7-day hold, and payouts go out weekly.
  • Cash out from $10.
  • If you brought a creator in with your code, you also receive a referral bonus of 10% of their ticket sales in their first 30 days — taken from the creator’s share, direct inviter only.
